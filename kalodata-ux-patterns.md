# KaloData UX Patterns (Observed)

## Navigation
- Top horizontal nav: Explore, Category, Shop, Creator, Product, Video & Ad, Livestream
- Each nav item is a distinct research vertical (like our: Dashboard, Analytics, Library, History)
- Clean white background, minimal chrome

## Product Discovery Page
- Left sidebar: hierarchical category filters (checkboxes, expandable tree)
- Main area: product list with thumbnail images, revenue, sales count, unit price
- Each row has a mini sparkline chart showing revenue trend
- Clicking a product opens detail view with full analytics

## Key UX Patterns
1. **Thumbnail + Sparkline in every row** — even in list view, each item has a mini trend chart
2. **Filters on left sidebar** — dates, categories, price range, follower count
3. **Revenue/Sales/Price as key metrics** — displayed inline on every card
4. **Detail popup/page** — click any item → full analytics with:
   - Revenue chart over time
   - Sales strategy breakdown (bar chart)
   - Related creators/affiliates
   - Contact information
5. **Competitor Insight** — side-by-side comparison of shops/products
6. **Ad analytics** — Ad View Ratio, Ad Spend, ROAS as key metrics

## What We Should Adopt for NYT Design Bot
1. **Product = Design Concept** — each concept is a "product" to discover
2. **Sparkline on every book card** — show score trend over runs
3. **Left filter sidebar** on Library page — filter by book, score, date, winner status
4. **Detail view with charts** — click concept → full analytics (score history, forum signals, images)
5. **Competitor Insight = Cross-book comparison** — compare 2 books side by side
6. **Revenue metrics = Score metrics** — socialMomentum, designNovelty, audienceSize displayed inline
